  • 拨锋 Glance — 在刀锋及身前一瞬按下架刀，拨开来刀并动摇对手重心；连段可连续拨锋。
    Press Guard just before a blow lands to turn it aside and shake the foe's Balance. Chain it through a string.
  • 踏锁 Bind-Step — 紫焰与笛啸预示无法架挡的突刺：迎着它向前踏步，压住刀身。
    A violet flare and a falling pipe-wail warn of a thrust no guard can stop: Step forward into it to pin the blade.
  • 刈斩 Reaping Cut — 按住斩键蓄力，刀锋闪出青光后松开；挨打会打断蓄力。
    Hold Cut to wind up, release once the blade flares pale blue. Being hit breaks the charge.
  • 失衡 Unbalanced — 对手重心满溢时靠近按 F 施展月轮。一直架刀会耗尽你自己的重心。
    When the foe's Balance fills, close in and press F. Guarding alone drains your own Balance.
